01

6-DOF Robotic Arm

Design, simulation & fabrication of a desktop robotic manipulator
SolidWorks ROS Arduino 3D Print
+
Overview

Designed and built a 6-degree-of-freedom desktop robotic arm capable of pick-and-place operations. Full pipeline from kinematic modeling in MATLAB, CAD in SolidWorks, to fabrication and control via ROS and Arduino Mega.

Technical Specs
  • Reach 420 mm
  • Payload 250 g
  • DOF 6 (revolute joints)
  • Actuators MG996R Servos
  • Controller Arduino Mega + ROS
  • Material PLA / PETG, Aluminum inserts
Outcomes & Learnings
Achieved ±2mm repeatability in pick-and-place task
Implemented forward & inverse kinematics from scratch
Integrated ROS MoveIt! for path planning
Presented at university engineering showcase

02

Autonomous Line-Following Robot

PID-controlled differential drive bot with sensor array
C++ PID Control PCB Design Solidworks
+
Overview

Built a differential drive robot with an 8-channel IR sensor array and custom PID controller for high-speed line following. Chassis designed in SolidWorks, custom PCB designed in EasyEDA, programmed in C++ on STM32.

Technical Specs
  • Chassis Custom laser-cut acrylic
  • MCU STM32F103C8T6
  • Sensors 8x TCRT5000 IR
  • Drive Differential (2x N20 motors)
  • Control Tuned PID (Kp/Ki/Kd)
  • Top Speed 1.2 m/s on track
Outcomes & Learnings
2nd place in inter-university robotics competition
Tuned PID manually — learned systematic approach
Designed and ordered first custom PCB
Lap time reduced 40% after control tuning

03

CNC-Machined Gearbox Housing

GD&T toleranced design through manufacturing on Haas CNC mill
SolidWorks GD&T CNC Milling Aluminum 6061
+
Overview

Designed a two-stage spur gearbox housing for a class manufacturing project. Created fully toleranced GD&T drawings, wrote G-code for a Haas VF-2 CNC mill, and machined the part from 6061 aluminum billet with three setups.

Technical Specs
  • Material Aluminum 6061-T6
  • Gear Ratio 4:1 (two-stage)
  • Tolerance ±0.05mm on bore
  • Surface Finish Ra 1.6 μm
  • Machine Haas VF-2 CNC Mill
  • CAM Fusion 360
Outcomes & Learnings
Part met all GD&T callouts on first inspection
Learned 3-setup fixturing strategy
Applied ASME Y14.5 standards throughout drawing
Received highest grade in manufacturing lab course
